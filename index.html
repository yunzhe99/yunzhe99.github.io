Biography

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin (UT Austin), advised by Prof. Lili Qiu. I received my Ph.D. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU, China), where I was advised by Prof. Hongzi Zhu and Prof. Lili Qiu. I was a member of the Advanced Computing Institute, led by Prof. Minyi Guo. I received my B.S. degree from Wuhan University in 2021. My research work focuses on Mobile Computing, Mobile Sensing and Internet of Things.
